Proclamation I · The Welcome

Of the Call

Each year, Year One included, a new order is drawn at random and shown on the Port clock. That order is the Order of Access. You may look at it; never touch it. Then, one by one, the Chairman calls each number to the Port. When yours is called, you go alone, and you do four things, always in this order:

  1. Ship Through. Look at the cards waiting on your Landside, in front of your Warehouse, sent by others. Send any of them on, Land-side up, to another Warehouse the conveyors allow. You may send your In-Hand card the same way. Whatever you do not send stays where it is.
  2. Open your Warehouse. Place your In-Hand card inside, if you kept it. Now you may ship any of your Warehouse cards to others, Land-side up, wherever the conveyors allow.
  3. Close your Warehouse.
  4. Take your new card from the Chairman, and return to this Chamber.

Cards that arrive on your Landside are not yours yet. When the last number has been called, the Chairman moves every Landside into its Warehouse. Only then are those cards yours to use, from next year on.

In your first year you arrive with empty hands and your Order Pass. Inside your Warehouse you will find your Industry and three cards. Remember everything.
