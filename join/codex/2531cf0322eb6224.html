Of the cards

Every Resource card has two sides.

The back shows a Land: Ocean, Mountain, Fields, Forest or Desert. When cards move through the Port, you will only ever see their Lands. Learn what each Land can hide. Your Reference Sheet shows it.

Your Reference Sheet tells you what your Industry needs and how it scores. Study it. You will not be able to check your Warehouse whenever you like, so remember what you hold. Do not bargain for what you already have, and never promise what you do not.
