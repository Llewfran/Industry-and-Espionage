Proclamation IV

Year Five

The Production Line · Read at the start of Year Five, before the first Call.

Industrialists, the year of commitment has come.

When you are called this year, you will Ship Through and open your Warehouse as always. Then, before you close it, the Chairman will ask you to begin your Production Line.

  • Choose five cards from inside your Warehouse, including the card you just brought in. If you have fewer than five that may be laid, lay them all.
  • Only Resources and Boom Time may be laid. Calamities may not.
  • Lay them behind your Warehouse, Land-side up, in a row, in the order you choose. Order matters: Boom Time copies the card laid before it.
  • Once laid, a card is permanent. It cannot be moved, removed or reordered.

In Years Six to Nine, you will add exactly one card each year, after your shipping. In Year Ten, the Decade ends: no shipping, one final card, and as many more as you dare.

Cards on your Landside cannot be laid. Only what is inside your Warehouse can build your Industry.

The Chairman will answer any question of law. Choose well.
