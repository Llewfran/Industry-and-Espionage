Proclamation II · The Honour of the Pin

Of the shape of the Decade

Your Industry is built in a Production Line: cards you lay down behind your Warehouse, year by year, in the later half of the Decade. I will tell you more when the time draws near. For now, gather what you need, and learn who has it.
