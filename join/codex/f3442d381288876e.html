The Shape of the Decade

YearWhat the year holds
OneThe initiation. Initiates surrender their Order Pass at the Iron Gate and leave the Port as Industrialists.
TwoProclamation II, the Honour of the Pin, before the first Call: the Pin, the Cardinal Rule, the Trade Blocks. The Force Majeure is uncovered.
ThreeProclamation III, the Warning, before the first Call. This sheet is uncovered.
FourThe last year to gather before the Line begins. Know what your foundation demands.
FiveProclamation IV. After your shipping, lay five cards from your Warehouse into your Production Line, Land-side up. They never move again.
After FiveThe Herald speaks again. Sealed.
Six to NineLay one card into your Line each year.
TenThe final year. The Herald speaks before the first Call. Sealed.
The endThe Great Reveal. Every Warehouse opened, every Line turned. The standings read from lowest to highest. The Brass Torch.

Every Call

  1. Ship Through: Landside and In-Hand cards, as many as you wish
  2. Open your Warehouse: ship up to two
  3. Lay cards into your Line (from Year Five)
  4. Close, take your new card at the Iron Gate, return
At the chime, the next number stands ready at the Iron Gate. When the Port is open, one leaves and one enters.
