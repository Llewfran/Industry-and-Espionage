Proclamation I · The Welcome

Of Brasshaven and your place in it

Initiates, welcome to Brasshaven, the greatest city ever raised under steam and ambition.

For the next ten years, a Decade, each of you will run one of the city's great Industries. Banking or Fashion. Politics or Oil. Newspapers, Hotels, Weapons, Trade. Thirteen Industries exist in Brasshaven; not all of them are in this room tonight, and when six or fewer of you gather, International Trade and the Industrial Wonder stay sealed in the vault. Yours waits for you, sealed, in a Warehouse in the Port. You will learn what it is when you first stand before it. Tell no one. Or tell everyone, and lie.

Your number is your Warehouse. Keep it to yourself until it is called; your Industry you keep for the whole Decade.

Of the two rooms

You will live this Decade in two places.

This room is the Negotiation Chamber. Here you talk. You bargain, promise, flatter, threaten and deceive. Here, words are free, and nothing else is. No card may ever pass from one hand to another in this Chamber.

Beyond the Iron Gate lies the Port of Ironreach. There you act. You enter alone, only when the Chairman calls your number, and you speak to no one but the Chairman. What you do in the Port is final.

The Chairman is not a player. The Chairman keeps the Port, keeps the order and keeps the secrets. Ask the Herald, your Reference Sheet or one another first. Ask the Chairman here, and question and answer are heard by all; ask in the Port, on your own Call, and you may be answered privately. The Chairman gives counsel on the law, never on your fortune.
