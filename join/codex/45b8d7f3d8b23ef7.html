Proclamation III

The Warning

Read by the Herald at the start of Year Three, before the first Call.

Industrialists, two years have passed. Two remain before the Port asks something of you that cannot be undone.

In Year Five, after your shipping, you will lay five cards from your Warehouse into your Production Line, behind your Warehouse, Land-side up. Those cards will never move again. Every year after that, you will add one more.

Mark it well
Your Industry is judged by what lies in that line,
and by nothing else.

Look to your Reference Sheet. Know what your foundation demands. Know what scores. In two years, what is in your Warehouse is what you will have to build with.

The Decade continues.
