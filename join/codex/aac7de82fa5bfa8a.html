Proclamation VII

The Great Reveal

Read when the last Industrialist returns from Year Ten.

Industrialists, the Decade is over.

The Chairman now invites you all into Ironreach for the Great Reveal. Here is how it will unfold:

  1. Alliances. Pairs step forward, shake hands, and declare aloud. One handshake each; it binds. The Chairman records it without comment.
  2. The Reveal. In an order of the Chairman's choosing, each Industry is turned face up, then its Production Line is turned over, card by card. An Industrial Wonder is asked before each card, “Land or Resource?” The answer stands.
  3. The Foundation. The Chairman confirms whether each Industry stands. An Industry that fails its foundation scores nothing, and its Industrialist leaves the Port.
  4. The Reckoning. All of you return to this Chamber. The Chairman scores the Decade alone.
  5. The Standings. The Chairman reads every score aloud, from the lowest to the highest. The last name spoken carries the Brass Torch. Iron Pact partners at the top share it, unless one Industrialist stands alone on the same score.

In Ironreach, silence and decorum. The Port has one more story to tell.

Follow the Chairman.
