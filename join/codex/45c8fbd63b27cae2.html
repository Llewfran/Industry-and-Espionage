Proclamation II · The Honour of the Pin

A valid Trade Block combines both partners' scores, and both receive the total. But if either partner fails and scores nothing, both score nothing. Your partner, if they are in this room at all, is somewhere among these faces. To find them, you must hint at what you are. Hint too loudly, and the whole Chamber will know.

Of calamities

The Chairman now uncovers the Force Majeure. Every stranger card is explained there, and at the back of this Codex. Read it well.

Of the end

After the tenth year, every Warehouse will be opened and every Production Line revealed. The Chairman will read the standings aloud, from the lowest to the highest. One of you will carry the Brass Torch.
