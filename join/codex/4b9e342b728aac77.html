Proclamation VI

Year Ten

The Final Commitment · Read at the start of Year Ten, before the first Call.

Industrialists, this is the last year of the Decade.

The conveyors have stopped. There is no Ship Through and no shipping this year. When you are called, you will open your Warehouse, place your In-Hand card inside, and complete your Production Line:

  • Lay one card. This one is required.
  • Then lay as many more as you wish, from what remains in your Warehouse.

There is no new card when you leave. Your Warehouse is sealed for the last time. Whatever stays inside it stays forever, and the Chairman will count it.

Only one thing is still yours to change: who stands beside you. Before the Reveal, any two Industrialists who form one of the six Trade Blocks may declare their alliance by handshake in the Port. Each of you may offer one handshake only, and it binds. Choose your partner well. If either of you has failed, you both fail.

The last negotiation begins now.
