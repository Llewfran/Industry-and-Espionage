Proclamation I · The Welcome

Of the Iron Gate

The Chamber's clock keeps every Call. The Chairman opens Year One; from Year Two, when the reading is done, the Herald opens each year with the Herald's own PIN. Only the Chairman may pause it. When a year opens, you have a minute to settle while the first to be called goes to stand at the Iron Gate. Thirty seconds before a Call ends, a chime sounds and the clock shows the next number: whoever holds it stands ready at the Gate, and the one In Port finishes and prepares to leave. When time runs out, the Port is open: one leaves, the next enters.

Of trust

Promise freely. Deliver, or be remembered. Brasshaven forgets nothing, and a Chamber that stops trusting you stops trading with you.
