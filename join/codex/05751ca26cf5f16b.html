Proclamation I · The Welcome

Of stranger cards

Among the Resources move stranger cards. One brings prosperity: Boom Time. Others bring calamity, and Brasshaven knows five by name: Espionage, Theft, Depression, War and FIRE! Not every one walks the conveyors every Decade. One that finds you this year explains itself; after it, all will be told.

Of the card in your hand

Each time you leave the Port, the Chairman places one card in your hand: your In-Hand card, the only card you ever carry into this Chamber.

You may show it, front or back, to one person, to many or to no one. Describe it truly or falsely, or deny you have it. You may never give, trade, swap or lose it. The Chairman records it, and when you next enter the Port it must be the same card. If not, the card leaves the game, you may not ship that year, and you leave empty-handed.

Of shipping

Brasshaven's conveyors obey a law of their own, older than any of you. Not every shipment will be carried. Should the conveyors refuse your goods, the Chairman will tell you so, and nothing more. The law will be made known in time. Know this already: no more than two cards may leave your Warehouse in one Call.
