Force Majeure: the Stranger Cards

Boom Time
Boom Time

A blessing, not a calamity. Ship it, or lay it in your Production Line, where at the Reveal it becomes an exact copy of the card before it. Laid first in the Line, it copies nothing. Boom after Boom copies what the first became.

Espionage
Espionage

Play it from your hand on your own Call, before Ship Through, by showing it to the Chairman. Choose one other Warehouse and see the faces of its Landside or its Production Line. Then it is discarded. Stuck in a Warehouse, it costs nothing.

Theft
Theft

Play it during Ship Through, from your hand or your Landside, onto one Landside you may ship to. Take one card blind, by its Land; it goes to your own Landside, usable next year. The Theft stays behind, and its victim may play it on in turn. Stuck in a Warehouse: −1.

Uncovered at the Honour of the Pin, after Year One. Force Majeure travels like any card, Land-side up, by hand or Landside. Once inside a Warehouse it never moves again, and it may never enter a Production Line.
