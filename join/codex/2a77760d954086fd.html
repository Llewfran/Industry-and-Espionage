Proclamation II · The Honour of the Pin

Industrialists, the first year is done.

Look around you. Each of you left a Pass at the Iron Gate, and each of you came back wearing something you did not carry in. That is your Port Access Pin. The Chairman fixed it to you in private, inside Ironreach, and with it you ceased to be an Initiate. You are an Industrialist of Brasshaven.

The Port Access Pin

The Pin is not a trinket. It is your name in the Port. The Chairman will call you by its number and by nothing else. Wear it at all times, where everyone can see it. A hidden Pin is an insult to the Port.

And your Pin tells the Chamber one more thing. Some of you have already felt it in the Port, when the conveyors refused your goods. Tonight, everyone learns why.
