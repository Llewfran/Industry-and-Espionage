Proclamation II · The Honour of the Pin
The Cardinal Rule
ODD ⇄ EVEN
Odd may ship only to Even.
Even may ship only to Odd.
Never to your own Warehouse.

Look at the Pins around you. Half this room can trade with you. The other half cannot, except through someone else.

Some of you guessed it. Some of you made promises the conveyors could never keep. From tonight, the whole Chamber knows. Choose your friends accordingly.
