The five Developments

Every face carries one Development emblem at its foot. Learn them: across a Warehouse, an emblem is the quickest way to read a card.

Medicine
Found on Ocean and Desert cards.
Needed by Cruise Lines, Hotels, Politics (two), Oil & Gas, International Trade and the Industrial Wonder.
Agriculture
Found on Ocean and Fields cards.
Needed by Food Retail, Fashion (two), Property Development (two), International Trade and the Industrial Wonder.
Literacy
Found on Fields and Forest cards.
Needed by Banking, Food Retail, Hotels, Politics, International Trade and the Industrial Wonder. Newspapers scores by it alone.
Currency
Found on Mountain and Desert cards.
Needed by Banking (two), Cruise Lines, Food Retail, International Trade (two) and the Industrial Wonder.
Weaponry
Found on Mountain and Forest cards.
Needed by Oil & Gas (two), Weapons Manufacture (four) and the Industrial Wonder.
Five cards behind a Warehouse, all Forest? Someone is gathering Timber or Textile, or Literacy, or Weaponry. Count the Lands. Infer. Trust none.
