Proclamation V

The Silent Procession

Read after the last Industrialist returns from Year Five, before the Procession begins.

Industrialists, rise.

For five years the Port has received you one at a time. Tonight, and only tonight, the Chairman opens Ironreach to all of you at once. You are invited to walk past every Production Line in Brasshaven.

You will see only Lands. Five cards behind every Warehouse, Land-side up. Ocean, Mountain, Fields, Forest, Desert. Read them as you will.

The Three Laws of the Procession
Silence. Not a word, not a whisper, not a sign.
Hands at rest. Touch nothing: no Warehouse, no Landside, no card.
Keep walking. Pass each line once. Do not linger.

Whoever breaks a law is sent back alone, and named before the Chamber. What you have seen, you may discuss only when you return here. Then talk as much as you like.

Follow the Chairman.
