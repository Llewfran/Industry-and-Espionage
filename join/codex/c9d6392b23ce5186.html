Force Majeure: the Calamities

Depression
Depression

Moves by hand or Landside like any card. Once inside a Warehouse it never moves again. In your Warehouse at the Reveal: −3.

War
War

Moves by hand or Landside like any card. Once inside a Warehouse it never moves again. In your Warehouse at the Reveal: −2, but +2 to Weapons Manufacture.

FIRE!
FIRE!

Never moves. It comes only as the card you are handed at the Iron Gate: the Chairman puts it straight into your Warehouse, and you leave with no In-Hand card that year. No points lost. Nobody can check a hand: "the Fire took my card" may even be true.

Calamity penalties apply at the Reveal to every Industry except the Philanthropist, who counts each calamity only as a card. No score falls below nothing.
